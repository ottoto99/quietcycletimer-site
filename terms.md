Quiet Cycle Timer is provided by ottoto. The app is free to download. From the first launch, timer use is unlimited for seven days. After that, the free plan allows three qualifying timers per week, resetting on Monday. A timer counts once when a focus interval of at least five minutes finishes without being skipped. Shorter or stopped intervals do not count. Premium is offered as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Both plans remove ads and the weekly timer limit while active.
