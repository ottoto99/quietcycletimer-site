# Terms of Use / 利用規約

Last updated / 最終更新日: 2026-11-08

## English

Quiet Cycle Timer is provided by ottoto. The app is free to download. From installation, the free plan allows five qualifying timers per week, with each week running from Sunday through Saturday in the device's local time. A timer counts once when its combined focus and break running time reaches five minutes. Paused time and time skipped ahead do not count. Stopping before five minutes does not count. When no free uses remain, a new timer cannot be started. Premium is offered as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Both plans remove ads and the weekly timer limit while active. Prices are displayed in the purchase screen and charged through the App Store. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings. Free-use records are stored locally and may reset if the app is deleted and reinstalled.

Use of the app is subject to [Apple's Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/). The app's timer and alarm behavior also depends on your device settings and Apple's system services.
